# THM-0132 support — owner ratification, 2026-10-04

Finding `552cf4adc6d52c96` (high): `proxy.admission_currency_gate` was credited with showing the
§7 admission gate is CONSULTED, but its `paths` exclude the serving path that consults it.

## The ruling

> For `552cf4ad`, approve the exact change:
>
> Old: `supported_by = ["unit://proxy.admission_currency_gate"]`
>
> New: `supported_by = ["unit://proxy.admission_currency_gate", "unit://proxy.admission_gate_unskippable"]`
>
> Every other THM-0132 field must remain byte-identical.
>
> The rationale is that THM-0132's existing claim depends not only on the currency-gate
> behavior itself but also on the structural fact that the serving path cannot bypass that
> gate. `proxy.admission_gate_unskippable` is therefore legitimate supporting evidence for the
> theorem rather than a widening of its claim.
>
> Also land the associated comment correction to `proxy.admission_currency_gate`, provided it
> only removes the misleading implication that its own integration paths establish serving-path
> unskippability.
>
> Do not broaden either unit's paths and do not change the theorem wording.

— Mats Sundvall (owner)
